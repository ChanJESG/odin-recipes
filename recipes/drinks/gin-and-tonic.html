Gin and Tonic

A classic cocktail of gin and tonic.

Ingredients

  • 4 cubes ice
  • 2 fluid ounces gin
  • 4 fluid ounces tonic water
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 lime wedge

Steps

  1. Place ice cubes in a tall, narrow glass.
  2. Pour gin over ice; add tonic water and lime juice.
  3. Stir well with a long-necked spoon. Garnish with lime wedge.
